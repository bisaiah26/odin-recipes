French Omelette

Back to the Recipes page.

Description

The french omelette is a classic dish that comes together in only a few minutes! Its really simple in terms of ingredients needed making it a great dish to whip up if you're in a hurry.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs in a bowl until you can't see the whites anymore, add salt and pepper.
  2. Add butter to moderatley heated skillet until fully melted but not browned then add the eggs and stir rapidly with fork until eggs are softly scrambled but still loose enough to form a single mass.
  3. Spread the eggs into an even layer and scrape the excess around the rim. If its still too liquid cook undisturbed for a few seconds until it's solidified.
  4. Remove from heat and use fork to roll the omelette over itself to the edge of skillet, then push it back to the edge of the skillet.
  5. Hold the skillet right over your plater and roll the omelette on to it, serve and enjoy!
